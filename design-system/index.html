01 · Principles

Depth over decoration.

Water is a system, not a filter. Every effect has a job: establish depth, show a state, or acknowledge an action.

Layered, not flat

Sea, currents, caustics, glass, and drops. Surfaces sit at clear depths, and the interface never fights the background.

Motion with manners

Slow ambient currents, fast interface feedback. Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion and can be paused.

One honest accent

The cyan current means action and selection. Status colours stay semantic: green same, blue moved, amber review, red missing.

04 · Water

The theme is a place.

Move your cursor across the pond, click to send a ring outward, and change the current without reloading.

Ripple pond · try it

Glass over water

Working surfaces get a frosted pane. Ambient light stays visible, but content stays readable.

WCAG AA 18px blur
Sea

One fixed background gradient. No image assets, no layout shift.

Currents

Three SVG wave bands roll at different speeds. They pause with reduced motion or effects off.

Caustics

Five low-alpha radial gradients drift behind the app on a canvas. Screen blend keeps them light.

Drops

Pointer drips are throttled by distance and time; clicks get one larger, slower ring.

05 · Usage

Three lines to join the current.

Import the tokens, import the primitives, then set the tone on the root element.

Markup

<link rel="stylesheet" href="tokens.css">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="components.css">

<html data-water-tone="ocean"
      data-water-background="sea">

<button class="ds-btn ds-btn--primary">
  Reveal counterpart
</button>

Water surface

import { WaterSurface } from "./water.js";

const water = new WaterSurface(
  document.querySelector("#water-fx"),
  {
    tone: "ocean",
    speed: "flowing",
    intensity: "subtle",
    effects: true,
    caustics: true,
  },
);

water.setTone("lagoon");
Accessibility contract. Motion stops under prefers-reduced-motion, every control has a visible focus ring, status is never carried by colour alone, and the water canvas is decorative (aria-hidden, pointer-events: none).
